Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At OmniImage, accessible from our website, protecting your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by OmniImage and how we use it.

Client-Side Processing (Zero Data Upload)

All image processing tasks (compression, resizing, conversion, rotating, filtering) are executed locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas technology. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server. Your data remains entirely on your device.

Google AdSense & Cookies

We use third-party vendors, including Google, to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
